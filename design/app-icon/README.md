## Generation prompt

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: refined modern iOS app icon for MarginShot, a notebook capture app. One finished square icon, 1024x1024.
Primary request: Design a very clean, optically balanced symbol combining a clear capital M with a single intentional central eye. The M must read instantly as M: two upright vertical outer stems, two diagonal inner strokes meeting in a centered valley, strong consistent stroke widths. Absolutely symmetrical left to right. The center contains one simple horizontal almond-shaped eye, with a single small solid circular pupil precisely centered. Make the eye read clearly as an eye, not an accidental hole or a tangled intersection. The eye is formed by clean dark negative space within the mint M, and its mint circular pupil is isolated and obvious. Keep enough breathing room around the eye so every contour is distinct at small size. The overall mark should have a stable upright M silhouette, NOT a bow tie, NOT an hourglass, NOT an X. No interlocking ribbons, crossing bands, overlapping page shapes, little triangular holes, or arbitrary cutouts.
Style: exceptionally restrained flat geometric identity design. Very simple, confident, memorable. Two perfectly solid colors only: very dark petrol teal background #062F32 and pale mint symbol #C1FFE5. No texture or grain whatsoever, no gradients, no shading, no highlights, no shadows, no 3D, no bevels. Crisp vector-like edges. Broad strokes and a clear, generous almond-shaped eye opening. Rounded outer top corners only where they help the M feel friendly; otherwise exact simple geometry.
Composition: centered single mark occupying roughly 64% of a full-bleed square dark teal canvas. Generous equal safe margins, no frame, no baked-in rounded outer canvas corners. iOS supplies its own mask.
No text other than the custom M symbol. No camera or paper illustration, no captions, watermarks, decorative details, mockups, or sheets of options. Output just the one final icon.
```
